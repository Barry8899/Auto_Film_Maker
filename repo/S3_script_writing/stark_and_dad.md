# Script: Stark and Dad

## Scene 1: Intro
**Location:** Unknown / Memory
**Characters:** Tony Stark, Nick Fury (briefly)
**Action:** Tony reflects on his cold relationship with his father, Howard. Flashbacks or voiceover establish the emotional distance between them. 
**Dialogue:** 
*(Voiceover)* Tony: "He was cold, he was calculating. He never told me he loved me. He never even told me he liked me."

## Scene 2: The 1970s
**Location:** S.H.I.E.L.D. Base, 1970s
**Characters:** Tony Stark, Howard Stark
**Action:** Tony, having traveled back in time to the 1970s, is searching for the Tesseract. In a hallway or elevator, he unexpectedly runs into a younger Howard Stark. Tony is visibly shaken but tries to play it cool, introducing himself under an alias.

## Scene 3: Conversation
**Location:** S.H.I.E.L.D. Base / Outdoors
**Characters:** Tony Stark, Howard Stark
**Action:** They walk and talk. Howard reveals that his wife is expecting a child soon. He expresses deep anxiety and nervousness about becoming a father, worrying he won't be good at it and might put his work above his kid. Tony listens intently, his perspective shifting as he sees the vulnerability in his father.
**Dialogue:**
Howard: "I'm just... I'm worried I'm going to drop him, you know? Or worse, not be there."
Tony: "You'll be fine. You might make mistakes, but you'll do your best."

## Scene 4: Farewell
**Location:** S.H.I.E.L.D. Base Exit
**Characters:** Tony Stark, Howard Stark
**Action:** It's time for Tony to leave. He has gotten what he needs. Before departing, he turns to Howard, offering a word of encouragement and a heartfelt hug—a moment of closure he never got in his own timeline. Howard is slightly confused but accepts the hug.
**Dialogue:**
Tony: "Thank you... for everything. You're going to do great."
*(Tony walks away, leaving Howard looking after him.)*
